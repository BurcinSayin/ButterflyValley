# Yog-Sothoth Essence Feats

Just as the [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md) background feat is caused by the latent temporal essence of Yog-Sothoth scattered during the Temporal Regression rituals, that same cosmic essence can be channeled and applied to a variety of other techniques if trained properly. Consequently, all of the feats in this document require the [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md) background feat as a prerequisite.

Every feat here is **Rare**. Access is granted by possessing the [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md) background and having had that latent essence awakened — through training with the Eternal Vanguard, contact with a concentration of scattered essence, or a Temporal Jump. A character who loses their connection to the essence loses access to these feats, though feats already taken are not lost.

## General Feats

### Time's Resilience [reaction]
**Level** 3
**Traits** Rare, General, occult
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Frequency** once per 10 minutes
**Trigger** You take damage.
*You instinctively reject the reality of your injuries, reversing time just enough to undo the worst of the damage.*
You rewind your personal timeline, reducing the triggering damage by an amount equal to 2 + your level.

### Echoing Memories
**Level** 3
**Traits** Rare, General, occult, mental
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
*By briefly bridging the gap between parallel realities, you download the experiences of another you.*
During your daily preparations, you can access the memories of a parallel self to gain trained proficiency in one skill of your choice. This proficiency lasts until your next daily preparations. Since this proficiency is temporary, you can't use it as a prerequisite for a skill increase or a permanent character option like a feat.

### Chronal Step
**Level** 3
**Traits** Rare, General, occult
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
*By drawing upon the scattered temporal essence of Yog-Sothoth, you blur through the spaces between seconds, crossing ground in moments that never quite happen.*
You can cast *warp step* as an innate occult cantrip.

### Temporal Anchor
**Level** 7
**Traits** Rare, General, occult
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
*Your connection to the fabric of space and time makes you remarkably resistant to being forcibly relocated or having your personal timeline altered.*
You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against teleportation and temporal effects (such as the *slow*, *banishment*, and *quandary* spells, as well as teleportation-based monster abilities). When you roll a success on such a saving throw, you get a critical success instead.

In addition, you always know how much time has passed since your last daily preparations, and you immediately sense when your own personal timeline has been altered, rewound, or duplicated — though not by whom, or why.

### Glimpse of the End
**Level** 11
**Traits** Rare, General, occult, divination
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
*By slightly aligning your vision with Yog-Sothoth's omnipresence, you perceive hidden realities, secret passages, and concealed threats before they unfold.*
Your senses are permanently attuned to what hides in the gaps of the world. You gain the following benefits at all times:

* **Keen Discovery:** You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to Seek hidden or undetected creatures, secret doors, hazards, and concealed objects within 30 feet. If you are a master in Perception, you can also travel at your full Speed while using the Search exploration activity, instead of half your Speed.
* **Piercing Gaze:** Reduce the DC of your flat checks to target concealed creatures to 3, and to target hidden creatures to 9.

In addition, you can cast *see the unseen* as an innate occult spell once per day.

### Will of the Key
**Level** 11
**Traits** Rare, General, occult
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
*You channel the essence of Yog-Sothoth to directly shape and change magical energies through sheer force of will.*
When performing any space or time-related ritual (such as Temporal Jump), you can use your Will saving throw modifier in place of the normal magical skill modifier (such as Arcana, Nature, Occultism, or Religion) for any required checks. Your proficiency rank in Will saves also satisfies any minimum proficiency rank requirements for the ritual's skill checks (for example, master in Will saves counts as master in Occultism). You must still be at least trained in the ritual's listed primary skill in order to lead or assist in it. Because this power is purely instinctual, it grants no actual knowledge or understanding of the skills being replicated.

### Master of the Threads
**Level** 11
**Traits** Rare, General, occult, fortune
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md), Time's Resilience
*Your grasp over your own timeline strengthens, allowing you to rewrite your fate more frequently.*
You gain a second daily use of the Deja Vu reroll ability.

## Druid Feats

### Foresight of the Wild
**Level** 4
**Traits** Rare, Druid, Ranger, occult
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
*You perceive the immediate future of the ecosystem, sensing where stones will tumble, winds will shear, and branches will snap.*
You and allies within 15 feet gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against environmental hazards, weather effects, falling objects, and natural terrain hazards (such as rockslides, quicksand, flash floods, and extreme temperature checks). Against such hazards that make attack rolls, you and those allies also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC.

Additionally, when traveling in wilderness environments, you can use the **Search** exploration activity while simultaneously performing the **Scout** or **Avoid Notice** activity.

### Rewind Vitality
**Level** 8
**Traits** Rare, Cleric, Druid, Oracle, Sorcerer, occult, spellshape, concentrate
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Frequency** once per 10 minutes
**Cost** 1 Action
*You weave temporal essence into your magic, causing the healing to ripple forward in time.*
If your next action is to cast a primal or divine spell of 2nd rank or higher with the healing trait that targets a single creature, the target's timeline echoes the mending. In addition to the spell's normal healing, the target gains fast healing equal to the spell's rank for 3 rounds.

### Temporal Companion [reaction]
**Level** 10
**Traits** Rare, Druid, Ranger, occult
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md), Animal Companion or Familiar
**Trigger** Your animal companion or familiar within 30 feet is targeted by an attack.
*You shatter the timeline around your companion, causing them to flicker rapidly between seconds to avoid a blow.*
Your companion gains the **concealed** condition against the triggering attack and against every other attack until the start of your next turn (an attacker must succeed at a DC 5 flat check or the attack misses). If the triggering attack misses due to this concealment, your companion or familiar can Step as a free action.

### Coterminous Elemental Gates
**Level** 12
**Traits** Rare, Druid, occult, spellshape, concentrate
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Cost** 1 Action
*You align your consciousness with the Key and the Gate, opening microscopic tears through the spaces between dimensions to deliver elemental destruction instantaneously.*
If your next action is to cast a primal spell that deals acid, cold, electricity, or fire damage, choose one of the following effects to apply to the spell:

* **Spatial Bypass:** The spell treats greater cover as standard cover, and standard cover as lesser cover. It ignores lesser cover entirely (you still require line of sight to the target or area).
* **Fractured Burst:** If the spell creates a burst with a radius of at least 20 feet, you can split it into two separate, non-overlapping bursts, each with half the original radius, rounded down to the nearest 5 feet. Both bursts must have their origin points within the spell's normal range.
* **Glance of the Gate:** Creatures that **critically fail** their saving throw against the spell become **frightened 1** (or **dazzled** for 1 round if they are immune to fear).

## Cleric Feats

### Paradoxical Healing
**Level** 4
**Traits** Rare, Cleric, occult, healing
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
*Your healing magic pulls on the vitality of a timeline where your ally was never harmed, creating a buffer against future injuries.*
When you use your Divine Font to cast a *heal* spell that targets a single creature, that creature gains temporary Hit Points equal to double the spell's rank. These temporary Hit Points last for 1 minute.

### Aura of Temporal Distortion
**Level** 8
**Traits** Rare, Cleric, occult, aura
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
*Your most powerful divine magic warps the local timestream, causing the air itself to feel thick and sluggish to your foes.*
When you cast a spell using your Divine Font, you create a 15-foot emanation centered on you that lasts until the start of your next turn. Enemies within this emanation treat the area as difficult terrain.

### Stagnant Decay
**Level** 10
**Traits** Rare, Cleric, occult, spellshape, concentrate, divine
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Cost** 1 Action
*You weave the freezing essence of the space between dimensions into your destructive magic.*
If your next action is to cast a *harm* spell from your divine font targeting a single creature, time slows to a crawl around them. The target's result on its Fortitude save against the spell also determines the following:

* **Critical Success:** The creature is unaffected.
* **Success:** The creature is unaffected.
* **Failure:** The creature is **slowed 1** for 1 round.
* **Critical Failure:** The creature is **slowed 1** for 1 minute.

### Avatar of the Key
**Level** 12
**Traits** Rare, Cleric, occult, spellshape, concentrate
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Cost** 1 Action
*You temporarily manifest a fraction of Yog-Sothoth's essence, allowing your magic to reach targets through the spaces between conventional reality.*
If your next action is to cast a spell, that spell ignores circumstance bonuses to the targets' AC and saving throws, and you treat targets with standard cover as having no cover against the spell.

## Fighter Feats

### Predictive Aim
**Level** 4
**Traits** Rare, Fighter, Gunslinger, Ranger, occult, divination, attack
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Cost** 2 Actions
*You peer into the immediate future to track your target's exact movements before they even make them.*
Make a ranged Strike. This Strike ignores concealment, and the target is **off-guard** against it. If the Strike hits, the target remains off-guard until the start of your next turn.

### Strike from the Future
**Level** 8
**Traits** Rare, Fighter, Barbarian, Ranger, Swashbuckler, occult, press, fortune, attack
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Requirements** You are not **off-guard** to the target of the Strike.
**Cost** 1 Action
*You pull a moment of clarity from your own future to strike exactly when the enemy's guard is down.*
Make a melee Strike. You can roll the attack twice and use the better result, applying your multiple attack penalty normally. However, peeking ahead leaves your present defense exposed: you become **off-guard** until the start of your next turn.

### Chronal Parry [reaction]
**Level** 10
**Traits** Rare, Fighter, Monk, Swashbuckler, occult, move
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Trigger** A creature targets you with a melee or ranged attack, and you can see the attacker.
*By accelerating your personal timestream for a split second, you slip between frames of reality to evade or redirect incoming strikes.*
You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses, you can Step or Stride up to 10 feet as part of this reaction.

**Special** If you have the Nimble Dodge feat, the circumstance bonus to AC from *Chronal Parry* and from Nimble Dodge each increase to +3. If you have Deflect Projectile (or Cut from the Air), you can use that reaction against ranged spell attack rolls as well as against physical projectiles.

### Timeline Severance
**Level** 12
**Traits** Rare, Fighter, Barbarian, Champion, Ranger, occult, flourish, incapacitation, attack
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Cost** 1 Action
*You slip a rapid strike through an enemy's future trajectory to disrupt their momentum.*
Make a Strike with a weapon or unarmed attack (melee or ranged). If it hits, the target must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC. If your Strike was a critical hit, the target's save result is one degree worse than the result it rolled.

* **Critical Success:** The creature is unaffected.
* **Success:** The creature is **off-guard** against the next attack made against it before the end of your current turn.
* **Failure:** The creature is **slowed 1** for 1 round.
* **Critical Failure:** The creature is **slowed 2** for 1 round.

## Champion Feats

### Shield of Foresight
**Level** 8
**Traits** Rare, Champion, occult, divination
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
*You broadcast a split-second vision of impending harm directly into an ally's mind, giving them the exact forewarning needed to defend themselves.*
You can use your Champion's Reaction when an enemy targets your ally with an attack, rather than waiting for that enemy to deal damage. Both the enemy and your ally must still be in your champion's aura, and you must still meet all other requirements for your Champion's Reaction. When you do, the ally gains a +1 circumstance bonus to their AC against the triggering attack, in addition to the normal effects of your Champion's Reaction.

Because you are reacting to a blow that hasn't landed yet, any part of your Champion's Reaction that makes a Strike against the triggering enemy, deals damage to it, or **moves a creature** resolves **after** the triggering attack has been resolved. Resistance and other protections your Champion's Reaction grants your ally still apply to the triggering attack normally.

### Dimensional Lockdown
**Level** 8
**Traits** Rare, Champion, Fighter, Monk, Ranger, occult, attack, flourish
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Cost** 1 Action
*Your strikes carry the anchoring weight of Yog-Sothoth, pinning a creature's essence to their current coordinates in space.*
Make a melee weapon Strike. If it hits, the target must attempt a Will save against your class DC; if your Strike was a critical hit, the target's save result is one degree worse than the result it rolled. The creature is then temporarily immune to this effect for 1 minute.

* **Critical Success:** The creature is unaffected.
* **Success:** The creature's dimensional anchor fluctuates. If it attempts to use a teleportation or extradimensional effect before the end of its next turn, it must succeed at a DC 11 flat check or the action is disrupted and lost.
* **Failure:** The creature can't use teleportation or extradimensional movement effects for 1 round.
* **Critical Failure:** As failure, but the duration increases to 1 minute.

### Rewind the Fall [reaction]
**Level** 10
**Traits** Rare, Champion, Cleric, Oracle, occult, fortune, misfortune
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Frequency** once per day
**Trigger** An ally within 30 feet is reduced to 0 Hit Points by an enemy's attack roll or a failed saving throw.
*When a fatal blow lands, you violently reject that timeline, rewinding reality to offer your ally a second chance at survival.*
You rewind the last few seconds. If triggered by an attack, the attacker must reroll the attack roll and use the lower result. If triggered by a saving throw, the ally can reroll the save and use the higher result. If the new result keeps the ally above 0 Hit Points, they don't fall unconscious or drop their items.

### Chronal Interception
**Level** 12
**Traits** Rare, Champion, occult, move
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
*You step briefly outside the flow of time, traversing the battlefield in an instant to protect an ally who would otherwise be out of reach.*
When an ally within 30 feet of you but outside your champion's aura would trigger your Champion's Reaction if they were inside it, you can use your Champion's Reaction. You can Stride up to 10 feet as a free action before the triggering attack resolves. You must end this Stride in a position where you can legally use your Champion's Reaction.

## Rogue Feats

### Temporal Feint
**Level** 6
**Traits** Rare, Rogue, Investigator, Swashbuckler, occult, mental
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Cost** 1 Action
*You send a momentary echo of yourself a few seconds into the future to distract your foe with an attack that hasn't happened yet.*
Attempt a Deception check against the Perception DC of a target within 15 feet. Unlike a normal Feint, the target does not need to be within your melee reach.

* **Critical Success:** The target is **off-guard** against your melee and ranged attacks until the end of your next turn.
* **Success:** The target is **off-guard** against the next melee or ranged attack you make against it before the end of your current turn.
* **Failure:** The target sees through the echo, and you take no other effect.
* **Critical Failure:** Your echo arrives late and telegraphs your real position. You are **off-guard** against melee and ranged attacks the target makes against you until the end of your next turn.

### Temporal Sidestep [reaction]
**Level** 4
**Traits** Rare, Rogue, Investigator, Monk, Ranger, Swashbuckler, occult, move
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Trigger** You succeed at a Reflex save.
*You dodge with such impossible speed that you actually arrive in a micro-second of the future, completely avoiding the danger's wake.*
You Step.

### Stolen Moment [free action]
**Level** 8
**Traits** Rare, Rogue, Investigator, Ranger, Swashbuckler, occult, move
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Trigger** You roll initiative.
*You perceive the outbreak of violence a fraction of a second before anyone else, allowing you to steal the first moment of a fight.*
You Stride or Step before the first turn in the initiative order begins. If you end this movement in cover or concealment, you can attempt to Hide as a free action. If you use this ability, you are **slowed 1** on your first turn as the stolen time catches up to you.

### Phase Strike
**Level** 10
**Traits** Rare, Rogue, Fighter, Magus, Ranger, occult, flourish, teleportation, attack
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
**Cost** 1 Action
*You open a momentary spatial tear, extending your weapon through the spaces between dimensions to strike from a completely unexpected angle.*
Choose an unoccupied square within 30 feet of you that is adjacent to a creature you can see. Make a melee Strike against that creature, treating your attack as originating from the chosen square; your weapon's reach is measured from that square rather than from your own space. If an ally is positioned such that they and the chosen square would flank the target, the target is **off-guard** against this Strike.

### Strike Before Time
**Level** 12
**Traits** Rare, Rogue, Investigator, Ranger, Swashbuckler, occult
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
*You are always already moving before the timeline officially dictates a battle has begun.*
At the start of your first turn in every combat, you are **quickened 1**, and you remain quickened 1 through the end of your second turn. You can use the extra action only to Stride, Step, Hide, or make a Strike.

## Inquisitor Feats

### Gaze of the Key [reaction]
**Level** 6
**Traits** Rare, Inquisitor, occult, mental, visual
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md), Stern Gaze
**Trigger** You use Stern Gaze.
*You force your enemy to glimpse the terrifying, infinite vastness of Yog-Sothoth's realm through your eyes, shattering their perception of space and time.*
Instead of the normal effects of Stern Gaze, the target must attempt a Will save against your spell DC.

* **Critical Success:** The creature is unaffected.
* **Success:** The creature is **dazzled** for 1 round.
* **Failure:** The creature is **dazzled** and **off-guard** for 1 round.
* **Critical Failure:** The creature is **dazzled** and **off-guard** for 1 round, and is **frightened 1**.

### Chronal Stratagem
**Level** 8
**Traits** Rare, Inquisitor, Investigator, occult, teamwork
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md)
*You impart fragments of your temporal awareness to your allies, granting them a supernatural intuition for exactly when to strike.*
When you review stratagems with your allies during your daily preparations, those who participated gain a +2 circumstance bonus to initiative rolls until your next daily preparations. Additionally, once per day, one of those allies can Step as a free action before their first turn begins.

### Judgement of the Gate
**Level** 10
**Traits** Rare, Inquisitor, occult, spellshape, teleportation, concentrate, move
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md), judgement spells
**Cost** 1 Action
*As you cast your judgment upon your foes, you slip through the spaces between dimensions to deliver it personally.*
If your next action is to cast a spell with the **judgement** trait, you can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see before casting the spell. If the spell requires a melee weapon Strike, you must end this teleport within your normal melee reach of the target.

### Terror out of Time
**Level** 12
**Traits** Rare, Inquisitor, occult, mental, emotion, fear
**Prerequisites** [Deja Vu](DejaVu.md), master in Intimidation
*You infuse your terrifying presence with the mind-bending reality of Yog-Sothoth, causing an enemy's timeline to endlessly loop their moment of absolute panic.*
Each time the frightened condition of a creature you inflicted it on would decrease at the end of that creature's turn, it must attempt a Will save against your spell DC. On a failure, its frightened value does not decrease that turn. The creature attempts a new save each round, and a success ends this effect for the current instance of the frightened condition.
