### 1. Chronological Events

* **Youth & Downfall in Oppara (Taldor):**
  * Born as the sole heir to the wealthy Vellorian elven merchant house.
  * Manifested the **Déjà Vu** condition (temporal premonitions), making him a prime target for Vanguard imprisonment and vivisection.
  * His parents depleted the entire family fortune on bribes to corrupt Justiciars to hide him.
  * Crushed by guilt and temporal dissonance, Eldrin fell into severe alcoholism and gambling debts.
  * Creditors went unpaid and sold his secret directly to the Eternal Vanguard.

* **Capture & Resistance Extraction:**
  * Justiciars arrested Eldrin and placed him in antimagic restraints.
  * A Temporal Resistance squad from Butterfly Valley ambushed the convoy and extracted him to **The Bastion**.

* **Service in Field Cell 7 ("Chronosfall Strike & Decipherment"):**
  * Stationed at the Mountain Facilities as the cell’s **Universalist Mage, Arcane Researcher, and Decipherer** (alongside a frontline guardian and an infiltration scout).
  * Executed four major ground operations across Golarion.

* **The Fall of Bastion City:**
  * Returning from the Mana Wastes, Cell 7 found Bastion City overthrown in a coup led by the **Shadow Weavers** and traitor **Ash Blackthorn**.
  * Cell 7 evaded corrupted guards and discovered that ~47 survivors escaped north into Butterfly Valley carrying **Ariadne Timeweaver’s** stasis-preserved body.

* **The Northern March & Tragedy at the Clockwork City:**
  * **Refugee Camp:** Located the hidden encampment; learned the main strike team had already pushed north.
  * **Forest Clan:** Aided the northern wild clan in slaying a mutated, temporally corrupted treant, earning clan membership, tokens, and guidance.
  * **Clockwork City Ambush:** Infiltrating the concentric construct city, an ally panicked and transmitted an Alkenstar construct override cipher against Eldrin's warnings.
  * **Catastrophic Failure:** The ciphers (designed for crude Vanguard copies) were flagged as hostile intrusions by the ancient Thassilonian originals, triggering the master alarm.
  * **Sole Survivor:** The squad was overwhelmed and sacrificed themselves so Eldrin could escape. Eldrin dashed through an open runic door into the **Central Pillar**, where he now shelters.

### 2. Recovered Lore & Intelligence (Info)

* **Operation 1: Kyonin (Sunken Vault of Aethel's Vigil)**
  * Decrypted notes of **Auramesties** revealing he investigated **Yog-Sothoth** as the cosmic entity behind Vanguard chronomancy.
  * Recovered records of **Milani's** rebellion and ambushes against Vanguard research convoys.
  * Discovered the Vanguard signed a secret non-aggression pact with demon lord **Treerazer** in 2560 AV to force elven submission.
  * Noted Kyonin declared independence in 4718 AV during the Whispering Tyrant crisis.

* **Operation 2: Ustalav/Fangwood (Whisperhold Archives)**
  * **Project Eternal Hope:** EV fused Starstone fragments into undead to make super-soldiers; one experimental subject, the **"Blazing Cavalier"**, breached containment and escaped.
  * **Project Broken Sword:** EV built divine siphon arrays (targeting Ragathiel); eliminated **Iomedae** at the Silent Glade with a forbidden *Temporal Regression* kill-team when she discovered the facility.
  * EV maintains a non-interference treaty with the ancient green dragon **Zedoran** in exchange for toxic alchemical spores.

* **Operation 3: Virlych (Gallowspire Ward Recon)**
  * Gallowspire's seal failed due to Vanguard bureaucratic decay, negligence, and chronomancy instability; EV garrisons fled, leaving the Orcs of Belkzen to hold the line.
  * Surviving knights formed the **Knights of Lastwall**; most lost their divine spells (proving EV had siphoned/regulated them).
  * **Divine Anomaly:** A rare few knights are miraculously channeling **genuine, authentic divine power** independent of the Vanguard.

* **Operation 4: Mana Wastes (Anomaly Suture)**
  * Vanguard temporal stasis fields completely fail in dead-magic/antimagic zones.
  * Alkenstar is an EV construct factory using blueprints acquired from **Runelord Karzoug**.
  * EV is covertly sabotaging Thassilonian researchers searching for the **8th Sin Rune**.

* **Strike Team Exploits Studied by Eldrin:**
  * *Sandpoint:* Thwarted a Vanguard operation and disrupted Karzoug's alliance.
  * *Mwangi:* Recovered uncorrupted pre-Vanguard history from the Ekujae elves.
  * *Kintargo:* Stole ancient Time Travel ritual tomes from Cheliax (the Resistance's sole remaining method of temporal travel).

### 3. Keynotes & Current State

* **Identity & Archetype:** Eldrin Vellorian — Elven Universalist Wizard / Arcane Researcher / Decipherer.
* **Psychological Profile:** Cynical, weary, pragmatic realist; scarred by addiction, loss of family fortune, and the death of his squad. Views the strike team as fellow hardened survivors rather than legendary heroes.
* **Key Tactical Knowledge:**
  * Understands the mathematical vectors for the [Temporal Jump](../Ritual/Temporal_Jump.md) ritual.
  * Knows firsthand that modern Vanguard construct ciphers backfire against the ancient Thassilonian originals in the Clockwork City.
* **Immediate Situation:** Low on spells and supplies, sheltered inside the Central Pillar after escaping construct pursuit through an open runic door, ready to rendezvous with the main strike team (**Esfir, Gerard, Surin, Veyra, Han**).
