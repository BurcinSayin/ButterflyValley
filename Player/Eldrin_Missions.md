# Welcome to the Field, Eldrin Vellorian

## Your World & Field Cell

Welcome to Golarion in the year 4723 AV (After Vanguard). You were born during "The Stagnant Era," an epoch defined by the rigid, monolithic rule of the [Eternal Vanguard](../General/Religion.md). As an elf possessing the ["Deja Vu"](../Feats/DejaVu.md) condition, you were rescued from Vanguard containment and brought to the [Bastion](../Organisation/Butterfly_Valley/Bastion_Governance.md) beneath the Chronosfall Peaks.

Because of your intellect, mastery of **Universal Magic**, and keen deciphering abilities, you were assigned to **Mountain Vault Field Cell 7** as the squad's **Arcane Researcher and Decipherer**. Operating alongside your frontline guardian and infiltration scout, your cell undertook perilous reconnaissance and archival recovery missions across fragmented timelines, forgotten ruins, and deep Vanguard administrative sectors before the march north to the Clockwork City.

### The Return to Ashes & The March North
*After completing your selected deployment above, your cell activated its return key:*

* **The Return Through the Rift:** Stepping back through the rift into Bastion City, you stepped into a nightmare. The Mountain Facilities were looted and quarantined, the Council of Time had been assassinated or scattered, and the criminal **Shadow Weavers** had seized control behind a terrified "Puppet Council." Worse, you discovered that **Ash Blackthorn** had betrayed the Resistance, wielding the *Temporal Dream Link* from Golarion to sow madness among the survivors.
* **The Escape North:** Refusing to surrender, your cell slipped past corrupted Bastion Guard patrols and melted into the Butterfly Valley wilderness. Detecting a colossal rhythmic pulse of temporal energy emanating from the unmapped northern reaches, your team resolved to follow the trail of the surviving refugees and the legendary strike team toward the **Clockwork City**—leading directly to the fatal construct ambush at the outer ring where your squadmates sacrificed their lives so you could reach the central tower.
