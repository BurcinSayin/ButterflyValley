# Welcome to Golarion, Eldrin Vellorian

## Your World

Welcome to Golarion in the year 4723 AV (After Vanguard). You were born during "The Stagnant Era," an epoch defined by the rigid, monolithic rule of the [Eternal Vanguard](../General/Religion.md). Every aspect of life—from the history taught in academies to the distribution of bread and justice—is dictated by the Grand Arbiter and the Stewards. For most, conformity is survival. But for those born blessed—or cursed—with the ["Deja Vu"](../Feats/DejaVu.md) condition, ordinary life is an impossibility. You are one of those few: an individual whose mind catches vivid flashes of events before they happen, echoes of timelines that were violently severed, and a gnawing certainty that the world around you is fundamentally wrong.

---

## Bloodline, Wealth, and Ruin (Oppara, Taldor)

You are an elf, the only son of the once-distinguished Vellorian merchant house of Oppara. In the gilded halls and bustling maritime bazaars of Taldor, the Vellorian name was synonymous with wealth, influence, and luxury. But when your Déjà Vu ability manifested in your youth—flashing with premonitions of trade fleet disasters days before they occurred, or recalling conversations with foreign envoys that had never taken place—your family recognized the existential threat. Under Vanguard law, any youth exhibiting temporal anomalies is seized for [special education](../General/Education_System.md), which is nothing more than polite terminology for forced isolation, indoctrination, and vivisection.

Desperate to protect their sole heir, your parents made a fateful choice. Year after year, the vast Vellorian fortune was siphoned into exorbitant bribes. Corrupt [Justiciars](../General/Judical_System.md), regional inspectors, and civic clerks were paid mountains of gold to falsify registries, overlook anomalous medical reports, and shield you from Vanguard scrutiny.

Yet privilege bought you neither peace nor safety. The jarring sensation of slipping timelines and the crushing guilt of watching your family's generational wealth bleed away weighed heavily upon you. To drown out the constant ringing in your ears and the dread of discovery, you fled into excess. You spent lavishly, drank the finest vintages, and gambled in the high-stakes parlors along Oppara's Grand Canal. What began as an escape soon became a trap. Debts mounted at a staggering rate. Unsavory loan sharks and underworld syndicates closed in on the Vellorian estate. 

When your family's coffers were completely empty and no more gold could be squeezed from the bankrupt estate, your creditors turned ruthless. Rather than cutting their losses, they sold your secret to the highest bidder—reporting the indebted, temporally unstable elf heir directly to the Eternal Vanguard.

---

## Capture and Extraction

The Vanguard’s retaliation was swift and merciless. Justiciars raided your quarters, stripped you of your finery, and dragged you into high-security containment as an unregistered temporal anomaly. Bound in antimagic restraints and facing imminent transfer to an isolated reconditioning facility, your life seemed forfeit.

Then, reality tore open.

Before the transport could depart, a squad of chronally attuned operatives materialized in a wash of temporal distortion. They moved with uncanny precision, disabling the Vanguard escort with practiced efficiency. They were Temporal Agents of the **Temporal Resistance** from [Butterfly Valley](../Organisation/Butterfly_Valley/Info.md). Recognizing your Déjà Vu resonance, they shattered your bonds, pulled you into their slipstream, and extracted you from Golarion before Vanguard reinforcements could mobilize.

---

## Life as a Temporal Agent: The Mountain Facilities & Field Cell 7

You awoke in the [Bastion](../Organisation/Butterfly_Valley/Bastion_Governance.md), a legendary demiplane sheltered outside the normal flow of time, nestled beneath the soaring Chronosfall Peaks. Here, free from Vanguard surveillance, refugees and rebels gathered under the banner of the Temporal Resistance.

Temporal Agents do not operate alone; survival across the timeline demands specialized, complementary teams. Because of your sharp intellect, formal education, and mastery of **Universal Magic**—a tradition that embraces all schools of magic without artificial limitations—you were assigned to **Mountain Vault Field Cell 7** ("Chronosfall Strike & Decipherment"). Operating out of the secure Mountain Facilities, your unit was a balanced tactical recovery team: a disciplined frontline guardian, a razor-sharp infiltration scout, and you as the squad's **Arcane Researcher, Decipherer, and Universalist Mage**.

Together, your cell calculated complex chronological vectors for the [Temporal Jump](../Ritual/Temporal_Jump.md) ritual and undertook four vital field deployments across Golarion and broken timelines to uncover the Vanguard's deepest historical crimes.

---

## Your Field Deployments & Research Discoveries

During your service in Field Cell 7, you completed four dangerous ground operations on Golarion. The intelligence, decrypted ciphers, and forbidden histories you recovered define your expertise:

### 1. The Sunken Vault of Aethel’s Vigil (Kyonin / Sovyrian Junction)
* **Operational Goal:** Infiltrate submerged pre-Earthfall elven ruins near the Tanglebriar border to extract astronomical star-charts and archives before demonic patrols overran the sector.
* **What You Discovered & Recovered:**
  * **Kyonin Partisan Network & Auramesties’ Archives:** You established contact with the Lantern Bearers resistance network and secured the confidential research notes of **Auramesties**, former First Speaker of the Winter Council. Deciphering his astronomical journals revealed that Auramesties was secretly investigating **Yog-Sothoth**—the cosmic entity of Time and Space—in a desperate effort to understand the unfathomable cosmic patron behind the Vanguard's chronomancy.
  * **Milani’s Lost Rebellion:** In a ruined forest redoubt, you recovered records of how the ageless rebel leader Milani fled the Vanguard’s *Celestial Lens* complex centuries ago and ambushed an EV logistics convoy carrying classified research into Ustalav.
  * **The Vanguard-Demon Collusion:** You uncovered suppressed archival proof that the Eternal Vanguard intentionally struck a secret non-aggression pact with the demon lord **Treerazer** in 2560 AV, allowing demons to ravage southern Kyonin to force the desperate elven nobility into a Vanguard protectorate.
  * **Kyonin’s Independence:** You confirmed that Kyonin declared independence in 4718 AV when the Vanguard was forced to redeploy garrisons to contain the Whispering Tyrant crisis in Ustalav.

### 2. The Whisperhold Deep Archives & Crimson Vigil (Fangwood / Ustalav Border)
* **Operational Goal:** Ambush an EV cleanup detachment sent to sanitize the Silent Glade crater in the wake of the Tyrant's Grasp crisis, seizing their lab manifests and classified route orders.
* **What You Discovered & Recovered:**
  * **Project Eternal Hope & The Blazing Cavalier:** Decrypted laboratory dossiers revealed Vanguard experiments fusing pulverized **Starstone fragments** into undead flesh to create compliant super-soldiers. You uncovered logs of a catastrophic containment breach: an uncontrollable, fiercely burning subject designated the **"Blazing Cavalier"**, who broke free and escaped into the wilderness.
  * **Project Broken Sword & The Fall of Iomedae:** You uncovered records of the Vanguard’s prototype divine siphon array, originally engineered to drain the celestial power of the Empyreal Lord **Ragathiel**. When Knight-Commander Iomedae discovered the forbidden facility and intercepted a critical Starstone shipment, Vanguard High Command sanctioned her elimination with a forbidden *Temporal Regression* strike team, resulting in the mutual destruction of Iomedae and the kill-team at the Silent Glade.
  * **The Fangwood Dragon Pact:** You seized Vanguard route orders revealing an official non-interference pact with the ancient green dragon **Zedoran**, who cultivates toxic alchemical spores for the Vanguard in exchange for absolute territorial sovereignty.

### 3. The Gallowspire Ward Recon (Ustalav / Virlych Border)
* **Operational Goal:** Deploy to the blighted perimeter of Virlych to decipher failing ward-stones anchoring the Whispering Tyrant's seal and evaluate the newly formed Knights of Lastwall.
* **What You Discovered & Recovered:**
  * **Decaying Starstone Abjurations & Bureaucratic Failure:** While the construction of the Great Seal by the Vanguard during the Shining Crusade is public history, your analysis of the failing runes proved the seal collapsed due to centuries of bureaucratic complacency, decaying garrison discipline, and abandoned maintenance oversight—exacerbated by ambient temporal instability from Vanguard chronomancy.
  * **The Birth of the Knights of Lastwall:** When the seal ruptured, the Vanguard garrisons fled in disgrace, leaving the Orcs of Belkzen to unexpectedly repel the Tyrant's advance. Disgusted by the Vanguard's cowardice and incompetence, surviving knights broke away, revived Iomedae's erased moral legacy, and forged the independent **Knights of Lastwall**.
  * **The Divine Power Anomaly:** You documented a profound metaphysical mystery: when the knights broke away from Vanguard oversight (the EV diversion), the vast majority lost their divine spells and blessings—proving their former abilities were artificially siphoned and meted out by the Vanguard regime. However, you witnessed a miraculous anomaly: **some knights are somehow still displaying and channeling genuine divine powers**. Without access to Vanguard siphoning, these rare individuals manifest authentic holy magic, tapping into spontaneous divine conduits or spiritual resonance outside of Vanguard control.

### 4. The Mana Wastes Anomaly Suture (Alkenstar / Spellscar Desert)
* **Operational Goal:** Deploy to the jagged border between Geb and the Mana Wastes to seal a violent temporal rupture and prevent a permanent paradox sinkhole.
* **What You Discovered & Recovered:**
  * **Dead-Magic Vulnerability:** You discovered that Vanguard temporal stasis fields fail completely in antimagic and dead-magic zones, proving that Vanguard time-control relies entirely on active ambient magic grids.
  * **Alkenstar Automata Factory & Runelord Pacts:** You uncovered that Alkenstar is a tightly controlled Vanguard manufacturing hub mass-producing clockwork constructs (automata, sentinels, soldiers) to patrol Geb without risking human personnel. The foundational blueprints were acquired through a secret pact with the Runelord Karzoug.
  * **The 8th Rune Friction:** You intercepted intelligence revealing hidden friction between the Vanguard and allied Thassilonian scholars: Runelord researchers steeped in Outer God lore are secretly hunting for the legendary **8th Sin Rune**, while the Vanguard is covertly sabotaging their search.

## The Fall of Bastion City: Returning With Your Cell

Your deployment in the Mana Wastes was your cell's final field operation before disaster struck. When your unit activated its return key and stepped back through the rift into Bastion City, you stepped into a nightmare. 

Bastion City had not fallen to a frontal Vanguard assault, but to a coordinated internal coup. Sleeper agents embedded within the population had struck without warning. The Council of Time had been assassinated or scattered, the Mountain Facilities looted and quarantined, and a terrified "Puppet Council" had ceded real control to the criminal syndicate known as the **Shadow Weavers**.

Worse still was the revelation of the mastermind behind the collapse: **Ash Blackthorn**, the Resistance's own Head of Temporal Operations, had defected to the Eternal Vanguard. From Golarion, Ash was now wielding the terrifying *Temporal Dream Link*, invading the minds of operatives and refugees alike, turning their own thoughts into weapons, and funneling endless waves of desperate refugees through the gates to destabilize the demiplane's temporal boundaries.

Moving in tight formation and using your squad's combined stealth and arcane wards, your cell slipped past the corrupted patrols of the Bastion Guard. On the city's fringes, you learned that a brave splinter faction of approximately 47 survivors and operatives—led by the field commander Ghost, the druid Owen Merova, and Sir Velinia Dawnblade—had broken through the cordon and fled into the Butterfly Valley wilderness, spiriting away the stasis-preserved body of **Ariadne Timeweaver**, the revered founder of the Resistance.

---

## The March North & Tragedy at the Clockwork City

Refusing to surrender to the Shadow Weavers, your cell melted into the wilderness. Guided by your arcane sensors and the persistent tug of your Déjà Vu, you detected a massive, rhythmic pulse of temporal energy echoing from the unmapped northern reaches of the demiplane. 

This was no ordinary anomaly; the readings were structured, vast, and rhythmic, suggesting cyclopean machinery awakening from a millennia-long slumber. Convinced that whatever lay at the heart of this power held the key to reversing the demiplane's destruction, your team resolved to push north across the frozen, untamed reaches.

After days of relentless tracking, your cell reached the outskirts of an impossible monument: the **Clockwork City**, a concentric metropolis of polished stone surrounding a colossal tower at its core. But the city was alive with danger: thousands of mechanical guardians—Citizens, Mages, and hulking Sentinels—patrolled the onion-ring streets with terrifying, mechanical precision.

You pleaded for caution, urging your squad to use the construct bypass ciphers you had deciphered in Alkenstar and study the patrol patterns from the perimeter before making a move. But disaster struck. Intrigued by the architectural wonders and hunting for shelter or supplies, one of your teammates broke discipline and slipped into an outer structure.

The intrusion triggered the city's alarm network in an instant. Ground elevators surged open, unleashing Clockwork Sentinels that swarmed the district with blinding speed. What began as a stealth infiltration collapsed into a desperate, running firefight. Your squad fought with everything they had, trying to push through the concentric streets toward the central tower and pillar doors. 

The constructs were relentless, immune to fear, and lethal. One by one, your teammates fell or hurled themselves into the fray to buy time for the rest to advance. In the end, the sacrifice was brutal and absolute: your squadmates were cut down by the clockwork horde. You were the only one who made it out alive—badly shaken, your spells nearly exhausted, and left entirely alone on the approaches to the central tower.

## The Strike Team: Legends, Survivors, and Exploits

You now stand at the threshold of the Clockwork City, ready to link up with the legendary strike team (**Esfir, Gerard, Surin, Veyra, Han**, and the memory of their fallen brother **Zstelian**). In the Mountain Facilities, their mission dossiers were studied not as heroic bedtime stories, but as masterclasses in brutal survival:

*   **The Swallowtail Festival in Sandpoint (Mission 1):** You studied the reports of how this team dropped into ancient Varisia to disrupt a Vanguard chronomancy operation. They prevented the staged massacre of the "Eternal Light" adventuring party, preserved critical lore, and shattered the Vanguard’s clean, uncontested alliance with the Thassilonian Runelord Karzoug.
*   **The Mwangi Living Archives (Mission 3):** You read their accounts of penetrating the deep Mwangi Jungle, where they navigated complex diplomacy with the ancient Ekujae elves and accessed unbroken, pre-Vanguard history preserved within the living trees—lore proving the Vanguard systematically falsified Golarion's past.
*   **The Kintargo Ritual Heist (Mission 6):** Their most vital operation. While Ash Blackthorn was orchestrating his betrayal back home, this strike team raided the fortified records halls in Cheliax to seize ancient Time Travel ritual tomes. Those books are the only reason the surviving Resistance members still retain the ability to travel through time and fight back.

### Your Perspective on the Party: Pragmatic & Cynical Equals

You do not look upon this party with starry-eyed reverence. You are an elf who watched his family’s ancient fortune turn to ash, who fell into debt and despair, and who knows that survival under the Vanguard requires blood and compromise. You recognize that every commendation on their record was paid for in close calls, scars, and the loss of allies like Zstelian. 

To you, they are not invincible saviors; they are **hardened, cynical survivors** who know how to stay alive and hit the enemy where it hurts. In an unraveling world where comfortable ideals are a luxury of the dead, they are the only people you can trust to watch your back.

## What You Know of the Big Picture

*   **The Eternal Vanguard (EV):** The absolute masters of Golarion, rewriting history and purging temporal sensitives to cement their eternal reign. Their crude manipulations are tearing the fabric of time apart, causing anomalies and storms across the cosmos.
*   **Butterfly Valley & The Bastion:** A fragile sanctuary built outside time to preserve free will. With Bastion City under puppet rule and the Shadow Weavers choking the population, the resistance is down to scattered embers.
*   **Ariadne Timeweaver:** The legendary founder who, according to Resistance history, built the Bastion as a haven for the free. Her stasis-locked body is now sheltered in the wilderness by refugees, her sleeping consciousness said to be whispering guidance toward the north.
*   **The Task Ahead:** The clockwork sentinels patrol the concentric streets ahead, and the answers to the demiplane's salvation—or destruction—lie inside the colossal central tower. Armed with your universalist spells, your decoded construct command ciphers, and the hard-won secrets of Golarion's frontiers, you step forward to join the vanguard.
