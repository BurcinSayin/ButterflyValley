# The Shining Crusade: Dossier of Colonel Raymond Caelan

> *"When the dawn horns blow, the court scribes in Oppara and the generals in the pavilion will see arrows, banners, and battle lines drawn across parchment. The men in the mud will see only the man standing to their left and the shield locked to their right. My job is to ensure that when the smoke clears, that shield is still standing."*  
> — **Colonel Raymond Caelan**, *"The Bulwark of Fort Lorrin"*

---

## Veteran Profile: "The Bulwark of Fort Lorrin"

* **Rank & Billet:** Colonel, Battalion Commander
* **Unit:** 1st Vanguard Battalion ("The Iron Sun"), 7th Vanguard Division ("The Sun-Watch") *(Late 3825 AV)*; formerly Senior Commander, 4th Taldan Heavy Foot ("The Sun-Forged")
* **Heritage & Age:** Human (Taldan veteran), 44 years old (24 years continuous frontline service)
* **Distinguishing Panoply:** Heavy Taldan field plate hammered true and recoated in rendered pork fat and wood-ash to create a matte-black anti-glare finish for midnight trench warfare; faded crimson crusader tabard bearing the Golden Sun of Taldor within which an Eternal Vanguard hourglass is hand-stitched; massive, scarred rectangular tower-pavise (*Sturdy Shield*) dented by skeletal maces; consecrated *+2 Striking Brilliant Longsword* ("Fort Lorrin Sun-Blade") stamped with the Fort Lorrin armory cartouche; bruised knuckles, square cleft jaw covered in thick salt-and-pepper stubble, hawk-like steel-grey eyes, and a crooked broken nose earned from an orc war-mace.
* **The Ground Reality:** An immovable moral rock and a frontline soldier's commander who lives in the same frozen mud as his men. Caelan harbors profound contempt for aristocratic vanity, courtly sycophants, and commanders who treat soldiers as expendable statistics on a map. He judges officers strictly by whether their shield-walls hold, their water is boiled, and their wounded are carried off the field before the rearguard withdraws.
* **Combat Premonition (*"The Echo-Sight"*):** In the heat of battle, Caelan experiences sudden temporal fugues—freezing for three heartbeats as vivid sensory premonitions wash over his sight. He perceives enemy assault angles, crumbling earthworks, and subterranean breaches hours before war-horns sound. Subordinates and frontline grunts revere this as the Dawnflower or the Vanguard's divine grace whispering battle-plans directly into his ear; to him, it is an unsettling, heavy weight—an eerie echo of violence he must bleed to avert.

## Personal Service & Frontline Highlights (3801–3825 AV)

* **3801–3810 AV — The Taldan Phalanx & Beachhead Consolidation:** Born in 3781 AV into a proud military lineage in Oppara. Enlisting in the imperial legions at twenty, Caelan is deployed to the northern shores of Lake Encarthan following the establishment of the **Vellumis** and **Vauntil** beachheads. Assigned to the 4th Taldan Heavy Foot ("The Sun-Forged"), he masters the brutal discipline of interlocking tower-shields and heavy pike phalanxes against shrieking waves of undead along the southern Ustalavic coast.
* **3811–3818 AV — Southern Ustalavic Redoubts & Rise to Command:** Rises through the ranks from shield-corporal to company captain across eight years of grueling trench warfare. Earns the fierce devotion of his men by standing on the forward fire-steps through bitter winter sieges rather than sheltering in rear command dugouts.
* **3818–3820 AV — The Red Crusader & Fort Lorrin's Foundation:**
  * **3818 AV:** Stationed on the forward breastworks when the Empyreal Lord **Arazni** descends to the mortal front. Caelan witnesses the sovereign celestial shatter the Vanguard's binding wards in righteous anger, choosing of her own free will to bleed alongside common foot-soldiers. The event permanently cements Caelan's belief that genuine command is defined by sacrifice, not dominion.
  * **3820 AV:** Promoted to field major during the construction of **Fort Lorrin** (the future Vigil). Oversees the fortification of defensive ditch networks, timber palisades, and munitions depots between Lake Encarthan and the Hungry Mountains.
* **3821–3823 AV — The Hungry Mountains & Vaishali Pass:**
  * Elevated to Senior Commander / Colonel of the 4th Heavy Foot. Coordinates logistical escorts, supply redoubts, and defensive ramparts through the rocky defiles of the Hungry Mountains.
  * **3823 AV:** Following the tragic fall of Arazni's avatar and the bloody counterstrike at Vaishali Pass, Caelan commands the heavy rearguard phalanx securing the retreat of battered divisions back toward Fort Lorrin. He refuses to withdraw until the final wounded carrier clears the defile.
* **3824–3825 AV — The Crucible at Pale Ridge:**
  * Ambushed in a rain-swollen, muddy sunken road by waves of dread ghouls, skeletal juggernauts, and bone-hulks, the 4th Heavy Foot is severed from the main column.
  * Caelan leads an 18-hour rearguard defense in knee-deep mud, locking shields alongside Kyonin assault master **Sergeant Major Aeluran Duskwalker**. Despite suffering severe wounds and watching over half the regiment fall, Caelan rallies the survivors and holds the perimeter until relief arrives.
  * Only 500 of 1,200 soldiers survive, cementing Raymond's reputation throughout the coalition as **"The Bulwark of Fort Lorrin"** and forging an unbreakable brotherhood with Aeluran.
* **Late 3825 AV (Present) — Gorcha Pass & The Canyon Approach:**
  * The 500 survivors of the 4th Heavy Foot are field-merged into the **1st Vanguard Battalion ("The Iron Sun")** under the 7th Vanguard Division ("The Sun-Watch"), with Caelan designated Battalion Commander alongside Colonel Vespera Morvath.
  * Dug into the freezing eastern mountain flank at Gorcha Pass, Caelan commands the forward lines, fiercely resisting Lord-Marshal Valerius Dawncrown's vain push into the Orphield River Canyon and shielding his men from Prelate Othemar's blind dogmatic zeal.
