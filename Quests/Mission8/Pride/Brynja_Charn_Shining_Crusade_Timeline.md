# The Shining Crusade: Dossier of Captain Brynja Charn

> *"The earth remembers every footprint, every drop of blood, and every drop of venom. The soil here does not sleep—it festers. If an officer cannot read the black rot on a pine root or the tremor in the shale, he has no right to order men into the mist."*  
> — **Captain Brynja Charn**, *"The Ash-Strider"*

---

## Veteran Profile: "The Ash-Strider"

* **Rank & Billet:** Captain, Chief Scout & Terrain Warden; Commander of the Ghost-Pickets (250 Light Foot)
* **Unit:** 1st Vanguard Battalion ("The Iron Sun"), 7th Vanguard Division ("The Sun-Watch") *(Late 3825 AV)*; formerly Reconnaissance Wing Commander, 9th Fort Lorrin Outriders & Sappers ("The Iron Wolves")
* **Heritage & Age:** Human (Kellid / Northern Ustalavic frontier ranger), 31 years old (13 years wilderness reconnaissance and frontline scouting)
* **Distinguishing Panoply:** Mud-caked +1 Shadow Resilient studded aurochs hide cured in winter tallow and peat-char, reinforced with carved mammoth-bone rivets; heavy oilskin camouflage peat-mantle woven with dried peat-moss, river-reeds, and bone-char twine that smells of pine resin and wet flint; +1 Striking Flaming composite shortbow ("Bone-Siphon Recurve") glowing with smoldering, smokeless peat-embers; +1 Striking Ghost Touch spear tipped with a 14-inch serrated mammoth-bone spike; cold-iron sickle; slit-aperture snow goggles carved from bone; climbing ropes and field medic's suture roll. Wide amber-gold eyes that dilate like an owl's in the dark; weather-beaten, frostbite-bronzed skin; and tawny hair woven with dried moss, rawhide, and raven feathers to disrupt her silhouette in the snow.
* **The Ground Reality:** A fierce, pragmatic wilderness stalker who understands the corrupted geography of Ustalav better than any imperial cartographer. Brynja holds deep scorn for rear-echelon commanders who plan advances from pristine parchments while ignoring toxic watercourses, seismic tremors, and necromantic ground-rot. She is ferociously protective of her scout runners (including Scout-Sergeant Matthew Brand and Scout Ryan), refusing to sacrifice them for empty vanity.
* **Primal Reflex (*"The Earth-Memory"*):** Brynja possesses an uncanny sensory attunement to terrain: pressing her bare palms to frozen earth, she slips into trances where she "remembers" the physical vibrations of marching boots or iron hooves that have not yet arrived, or claims she can taste the bitter ash of fires that will not burn until the next moon. Auxiliaries revere her as a skin-dancer who speaks with the bones of the land; in truth, her mind vibrates with latent temporal sensitivity, catching chronological reverberations echoing across the canyon floor.

## Personal Service & Frontline Highlights (3794–3825 AV)

* **3794–3812 AV — Northern Wilderness & Frontier Lore:** Born in 3794 AV in the rugged Kellid frontiers bordering northern Ustalav. Trained by clan wardens in tracking, survival, herbal lore, and reading primal spirits in blighted wilderness. Experiences early, disorienting temporal episodes where she instinctively guides her clan away from mountain ravines hours before catastrophic mudslides strike.
* **3813–3819 AV — Enlistment & The Ghost-Pickets:**
  * Drawn to the Shining Crusade by the creeping spread of Tar-Baphon's undead blight across ancestral hunting grounds, she enlists as a frontier guide.
  * Assigned to northern scouting detachments, she trains light skirmishers to move over frozen ash without snapping twigs, cross scree silently, and detect necromantic corruption by the smell of dead soil and water mold. Her scouts become known throughout the division as the **"Ghost-Pickets."**
* **3820–3823 AV — Fort Lorrin & The 9th Outriders:**
  * Stationed at **Fort Lorrin** upon its construction, Brynja is appointed Reconnaissance Wing Commander under Colonel Vespera Morvath in the 9th Outriders & Sappers.
  * Leads deep ranging patrols across the Hungry Mountains, mapping unexplored gorges, detecting subterranean ghoul warrens, and providing vital topographical data for General Arnisant's forward columns.
* **3824–3825 AV — Mountain Pickets & Corpse-Orchards:**
  * Ranks as the foremost scout commander in the eastern sector. Specializes in counter-reconnaissance, hunting enemy skeletal scouts and disabling bone-char alarm totems.
  * **Mid 3825 AV — Ambush at the Corpse-Orchards:** When the 9th is ambushed in the dark, Brynja leads the forward pickets in a silent counter-skirmish. Using her peat-mantle and smokeless flaming bow, she identifies safe paths through black-fog pockets, enabling Colonel Morvath's surviving 500 troops to break through the enemy cordon.
* **Late 3825 AV (Present) — Gorcha Pass & The Canyon Approach:**
  * Commands the 250 Ghost-Pickets of the merged **1st Vanguard Battalion ("The Iron Sun")** at Gorcha Pass.
  * Interrogates seismic tremors shaking the eastern ridge, deducing that the subterranean drumming heard by Scout Ryan signals hidden artillery movements rather than routine mining. Deeply concerned by the disappearance of her trusted scout leader, Scout-Sergeant Matthew Brand, in the fog of No-Man's-Land.
