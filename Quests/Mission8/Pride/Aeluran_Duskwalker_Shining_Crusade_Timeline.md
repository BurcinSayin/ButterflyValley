# The Shining Crusade: Dossier of Sergeant Major Aeluran Duskwalker

> *"The court scribes in Oppara and the prelates in Vanguard City write of holy banners, glorious charges, and divine destiny. In the trenches, the war is measured in duckboard rot, rusted laminar rivets, warped pike-shafts, and the horrifying turnover of short-lived men."*  
> — **Sergeant Major Aeluran Duskwalker**, *"The Iron Willow"*

---

## Veteran Profile: "The Iron Willow"

* **Rank & Billet:** Sergeant Major, Master-at-Arms & Heavy Assault Specialist
* **Unit:** 1st Vanguard Battalion ("The Iron Sun"), 7th Vanguard Division ("The Sun-Watch") *(Late 3825 AV)*
* **Heritage & Age:** Kyonin expatriate elf, 210 years old (27 years continuous frontline combat)
* **Distinguishing Panoply:** Blackened Kyonin fine-mesh chain shirt with laminar shoulder-guards, fitted with oiled-leather silent gaskets; twin serrated trench swords. Notched elven ears from crossbow bolts; silver hair gathered in a crown combat knot; cold, piercing violet eyes; and jagged violet-black necrotic scars across his torso and left shoulder.
* **The Ground Reality:** Aeluran harbors zero sentimentality for imperial propaganda or theological sermons. He judges commanders by whether they know how to brace a timber palisade, keep pikes dry, and preserve human lives. He holds deep contempt for vain nobles who spend soldiers' lives on personal glory.
* **Combat Reflex (*"Blade-Premonition"*):** In close-quarters fighting, Aeluran's senses anticipate enemy movements with impossible precision. He ducks, parries, and counters strikes before enemy weapons fall. Subordinates revere this as peerless elven reflex, while Eternal Vanguard war-chaplains officially explain his martial anticipation as a divine blessing channeled from the combined good will and unyielding martial resolve of the crusader ranks; to him, it feels like an eerie, cold curse of reenacting violent motions he has already lived.
* **The Post-Arazni Phenomenon (*"The Misty Realm Phase-Out"*):** Following Arazni's martyrdom in 3823 AV—where he took a necrotic blast shielding recruits—Aeluran began vanishing mid-drill into shimmering silver afterimages. Official Vanguard doctrine proclaimed that the host's combined good will summoned his spirit into the tranquil **"Misty Realm"** to mend his battle weariness, forbidding commanders from treating these absences as desertion. These phase-outs proved an extraordinary asset for the Vanguard: Aeluran returned healed to an extent as if he had fully rested for the time spent away, while his displacement let him travel and explore unhindered through physical barriers and lethal wards, perceiving branching near-presents of impending assaults and enemy strike angles.

## Personal Service & Frontline Highlights (3720s–3825 AV)

* **3720s–3780s AV — The Gilded Academy ("Special Education"):** Manifesting extraordinary combat anticipation during his elven youth, Aeluran was selected for the Eternal Vanguard's [special education](../../../General/Education_System.md) program for "gifted" individuals. He was enrolled as a privileged student at an elite academy—afforded premier martial tutelage, masterwork panoply, and high standing. Yet beneath the honors, he remained keenly aware of the quiet scrutiny: Vanguard observers monitored him from a polite distance, charting his uncanny reflexes and evaluating how his "blade-premonition" might best serve the state.
* **3785–3797 AV — The Tanglebriar Skirmishes:** Graduating into active service, Aeluran was posted to the southern perimeter of the Fierani Forest, skirmishing against Treerazer's demonic incursions along the Tanglebriar border. The savage, close-quarters fighting against abyssal fiends proved his combat premonition in real blood. Yet even in the field, the discreet gaze of Vanguard observers persisted, evaluating their gifted alumnus under live fire. In 3798 AV, weary of the gilded leash and perpetual surveillance, Aeluran volunteered for the grand imperial muster of the Shining Crusade—choosing the raw, unmonitored anonymity of the frontline trenches over a curated career under Vanguard observation.
* **3798–3801 AV — The Lake & The Surf:** Enlists in the 2nd Imperial Amphibious Cohort (*"The Iron Oars"*). Spends four brutal years escorting convoys across Lake Encarthan before leading the surf assaults that carve out the beachheads at **Vellumis** and **Vauntil**.
* **3801–3816 AV — The Fifteen-Year Slog:** Transferred to the 14th Taldan Heavy Foot (*"The River Wardens"*) across southern Ustalav. Endures fifteen years of grim trench warfare, watching human regiments cycle through generations of fresh recruits. Earns the moniker **"The Iron Willow"** for bending under overwhelming enemy charges without snapping.
* **3818–3822 AV — Carrion Hill & Arnisant’s Vanguard:**
  * **3818 AV:** Witnesses the Empyreal Lord **Arazni** shatter the Vanguard's binding wards in righteous anger, choosing of her own free will to bleed for common foot soldiers.
  * **3820–3822 AV:** Holds the ramparts during the grueling Siege of Carrion Hill (sparking camp rumors that he felled a skeletal dragon in single combat with his trench blades).
  * **Late 3822 AV:** Handpicked for **Supreme Commander General Arnisant's** central shock spearhead pushing into the Hungry Mountains.
* **3823 AV — Catastrophe in the Hungry Mountains:**
  * Deployed in the forward shock line when Tar-Baphon personally engages and kills Arazni.
  * When unholy shockwaves detonate across the defile, Aeluran shields two fallen recruits with his body. He takes a crushing blast of necrotic bone-shrapnel that caves in his armor and shatters his ribs.
  * Survives four months of agonizing surgery at Fort Lorrin. Medically disqualified from shock spearheads, he refuses a safe rear-depot desk and demands a frontline post, transferring to the 4th Taldan Heavy Foot under **Colonel Raymond Caelan**.
  * **Late 3823 AV — The First Phase-Out & Ecclesiastical Decree:** During his convalescence at Fort Lorrin, Aeluran experiences his first full phase-out, vanishing from his recovery cot for three days while still bandaged, leaving behind an empty bed smelling faintly of ozone and crushed pine. He reappears standing fully armored in the practice yard—healed to an extent as if having rested those three full days, while having traveled and explored the fortress perimeter unhindered by abjuration wards and mapped diverging paths of an impending infiltrator raid. Vanguard war-chaplains subject him to doctrinal review and proclaim that the combined good will of the soldiers he saved had pulled him into the **Misty Realm** to preserve his life, with Vanguard command officially embracing his unhindered exploration as a vital military asset.
* **3824–3825 AV — The Crucible at Pale Ridge:** Ambushed in a muddy sunken road, the 4th Heavy Foot fights an 18-hour rearguard stand against dread ghouls and skeletal juggernauts. Standing shoulder-to-shoulder with Colonel Caelan, Aeluran holds the perimeter until relief arrives. Only 500 of 1,200 soldiers survive, cementing an unbreakable brotherhood between Aeluran and Caelan.
* **Late 3825 AV (Present) — Gorcha Pass & The Canyon Approach:**
  * The battered survivors are field-merged into the **1st Vanguard Battalion ("The Iron Sun")** on the freezing eastern mountain flank.
  * Aeluran manages the trenches, inspections, and palisades alongside sapper Kellan Torpe, while enduring Prelate Othemar's hollow lectures.
